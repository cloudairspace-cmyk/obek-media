Services

What I bring to
your production

Whether you need a polished final cut, breathtaking visuals on set, or that perfect cinematic color palette — I've got you covered from pre to post.

Video Editing

Precision cutting, pacing, and assembly that transforms hours of raw footage into a cohesive, emotionally resonant story. From rough cut to final delivery in any format.

Cinematography

Bringing your vision to life through carefully composed shots, dynamic camera movement, and cinematic lighting. Every frame is intentional, every angle purposeful.

Color Grading

Setting the visual tone and mood through professional color correction and grading in DaVinci Resolve. From natural warmth to stylized cinema looks that define your brand.
